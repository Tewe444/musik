Soundeffekte in diesem Ordner
=============================

Alle Soundeffekte stammen von Kenney (www.kenney.nl) und stehen unter
Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal):
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

  "This content is free to use in personal, educational and commercial projects.
   Support us by crediting Kenney or www.kenney.nl (this is not mandatory)"

Die Namensnennung ist also freiwillig – wir nennen Kenney trotzdem in den Credits
(Menü und Buch im Spiel).

Herkunft (GitHub, CC0-Lizenzdatei jeweils im Repository):
- Kenney "Impact Sounds"    – https://github.com/Boyquotes/kenney-impact-sounds-for-godot
    footstep_concrete_*, footstep_grass_*, footstep_snow_*, impact_mining_*,
    impact_wood_medium_*, impact_soft_medium_000
- Kenney "Interface Sounds" – https://github.com/Calinou/kenney-interface-sounds
    click_002, confirmation_002, error_006, glass_002, maximize_006, drop_002,
    select_002, toggle_002
- Kenney "RPG Audio"        – https://github.com/Boyquotes/kenney-rpg-audio-for-godot
    handle_coins, handle_coins_2, book_flip_1, book_flip_2, book_open, book_close, cloth_1
- Kenney "Sci-fi Sounds"    – https://github.com/Boyquotes/kenney-sci-fi-sounds-for-godot
    explosion_crunch_000, low_frequency_explosion_000, force_field_000, space_engine_low_000

Die Originale (OGG/WAV) wurden mit Blender (tools/convert_sounds.py) in MP3 umgewandelt,
damit sie in allen Browsern laufen, auch auf dem iPhone.
